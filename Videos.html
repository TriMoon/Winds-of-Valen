Gallery Guide & Features

Sharing Tutorials

Use the form above to submit your own guides. Simply enter the video title, your player name, and the direct YouTube link to have it displayed in our gallery.

Categorization

Videos are automatically organized into categories: Combat, Skills, Gameplay, Guides, and Other. Use the "Quick Jump" navigation bar to filter and find specific content quickly.

Real-time Gallery

The gallery fetches content directly from the community database. As soon as a video is posted, it appears in the grid sorted by the most recent submissions.

Community Contributions

This gallery is built by players, for players. By sharing your footage, you help others master specific mechanics, locate hard-to-find drops, or optimize their builds.

Video Thumbnails

Each entry automatically generates a high-quality thumbnail from the YouTube link provided, ensuring the gallery remains visual and easy to browse.

Direct Access

Every video card features a "Watch" button that opens the tutorial in a new tab, allowing you to follow along with the guide while keeping your place in the gallery.

Safe Posting

Only valid YouTube links are processed. Ensure you copy the full link or the video ID correctly to guarantee that your tutorial is successfully added to the database.

Feedback & Support

Stuck on a mechanic? Browse the "Combat" or "Guides" sections first. If you have questions about specific videos, reach out to the player listed under "By:" in the video card.
